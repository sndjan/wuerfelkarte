# Emoji in Würfelkarte

The emoji images in this folder are from Google's Noto Emoji. They are used
as published by Google and are not modified. Würfelkarte is not affiliated
with or endorsed by Google.

## Animated emoji: `*.json`

Lottie animations from **Noto Emoji Animation** by Google LLC.

- Source: https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Static emoji: `*.svg`

Images from **Noto Emoji** by Google LLC (the static drawings shown when
nothing is playing, and for emoji without an animation).

- Source: https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji
- License: Apache License, Version 2.0, see `LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt` in this
  folder and https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

`scripts/fetch-noto-emoji.mjs` downloads both from Google's font server
(fonts.gstatic.com) and stores them here unchanged.
